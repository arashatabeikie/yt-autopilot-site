YT Autopilot

YT Autopilot is a private tool that its owner runs on their own computer to manage their own YouTube channels. It helps research topics, prepare short videos, and schedule uploads. A person reviews and approves every video before it is published.

It is not a public service. Nobody else can sign up or use it.

What it does with YouTube

  • Reads basic channel information and statistics for the owner's channels.
  • Uploads and schedules videos the owner has approved.
  • Updates titles, descriptions and playlists of the owner's videos.
